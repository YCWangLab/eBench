# Truth600 category audit

The experimental design contains seven biological categories, consolidated into three accession files only for execution:

| Processing group | Biological categories | Intended counts | Group total |
|---|---|---|---:|
| micro | bacteria, archaea, fungi | 351, 99, 50 | 500 |
| animals | mammals, other vertebrates, invertebrates | 15, 15, 20 | 50 |
| plants | plants | 50 | 50 |

The three bundled accession files contain exactly 500, 50 and 50 accessions. Direct membership matching against the bundled seven NCBI assembly-summary files confirms bacteria 351, archaea 99, fungi 50, plants 50, mammals 15, other vertebrates 15 and invertebrates 20. These are the authoritative category counts for this simulation dataset.

The accession panel itself is unchanged. The seven biological categories are consolidated into three processing groups only to simplify reference download and simulation execution; downstream reporting should retain the seven-category composition above.
